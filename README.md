# 🚀 Space Force 1.0

> **NASA Space Apps Challenge Submission**  
> An interactive, gamified aerospace simulation platform designed to make rocket science and orbital mechanics intuitive, engaging, and accessible to the next generation of space explorers.

---

## 🌟 Overview

A lot of kids dream of exploring the universe, becoming astronauts, or working for NASA, but traditional rocket science is often gatekept by intimidating jargon—like *thrust-to-weight ratios (TWR)*, *delta-v budgets*, *Max-Q*, and *pitch-over rates*—or dense, passive videos. 

**Space Force 1.0** bridges that gap by turning aerospace engineering into an immersive, multi-stage career simulation game. Players step into the shoes of a space station intern, guided by an AI mentor named **Astra**, learning real engineering and mission management principles through active gameplay rather than textbooks.

---

## 🎮 Core Gameplay Loop

1. **Telemetry & Anomaly Detection:** 
   * Scan live data streams (waveforms, thermal outputs, and gas/isotope monitors) against a completion checklist.
   * Filter out background space debris to identify high-priority anomalies originating from real celestial destinations: **The Moon, Mars, and Titan**.
2. **Vehicle Selection & Hardware Assembly:** 
   * Review mission briefings and hints to select the correct specialized vehicle (e.g., a Mars soil-sampling rover).
   * Tackle hands-on assembly tasks including wiring, welding, and valve adjustment.
3. **Risk Management & Budgeting:** 
   * Completed builds undergo strict screening. Faulty wiring, improper welds, or bad engineering choices trigger screening failures and **deduct from your limited budget**.
   * Run out of money? Answer aerospace trivia challenges to earn financial bonuses and bail your mission out.
4. **Rocket Design & Orbital Flight Simulation:** 
   * Graduate to full rocket architecture, balancing propellant loads, TWR, delta-v budgets, and pitch-over rates.
   * Launch your custom-built vehicle into a live simulation aiming for Low Earth Orbit (LEO) or interplanetary transfer orbits!

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **Interactive Mentorship:** Guided narrative progression with mentor Astra.
* **Dynamic Physics & Math Modeling:** Real-time simulation of rocket acceleration, gravity, atmospheric drag, and orbital mechanics.
* **Consequence-Driven Economy:** Budgeting mechanics that reward careful planning and penalize sloppy engineering.
* **Retro-Futuristic Synthwave UI:** Polished dark-mode interface styled with Tailwind CSS, custom canvas graphics, and FontAwesome icons.

---

## 🛠️ Built With

* **Frontend & UI:** HTML5, CSS3, **Tailwind CSS** (Synthwave/Aerospace aesthetic)
* **Interactivity & Simulation Logic:** Vanilla **JavaScript** (Real-time telemetry loops, countdown timers, and physics calculators)
* **Graphic Assets & Design:** **Canva** (Custom UI assets, badges, and mission illustrations)
* **Development Partner:** **AI** (Collaborative code structuring, logic debugging, and narrative design)
* **Data & Science References:** NASA Glenn Research Center (Rocket Equations & TWR), NASA Mars Exploration Program, and NASA Planetary Data System (Cassini Titan Science).
